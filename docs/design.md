# Display

The video pairs recorded source dispatches with decoded DSKY output. No values are supplied by the renderer.

| Reference | Applied convention |
|---|---|
| [Luminary 099, THE LUNAR LANDING](https://www.ibiblio.org/apollo/listings/Luminary099/THE_LUNAR_LANDING.agc.html) | Routine title, octal addresses, words and instruction columns. Source text comes from the saved listing map. |
| [LM DSKY assembly 2003994-091](https://ibiblio.org/apollo/2003994-091.html) | Annunciators at left; program, verb, noun and three registers at right. The keyboard is outside this display view. |
| [LM.ini](https://github.com/virtualagc/virtualagc/blob/ebd8695d23bde6eb9f26933ddf244b8d18987f21/yaDSKY2/LM.ini) | Two columns of seven annunciators, LM legends, channel masks and lamp states. |

Panel geometry, colors and bold Courier lettering are schematic approximations, not a mechanical replica. Listing text uses dark ink on a light field. Time, playback rate and fixture status identify the recorded test.

Playback: five seconds at the initial state; 0–10.4 s at 1x; 10.4–11.24 s at 0.04x; seven seconds at 1x; final state held. The last recorded source dispatch remains visible between events. Digits, signs, lamps and verb/noun blanking follow the saved output channels. No audio.
